# Remote Controlled Toy-Car



<img width="530" height="420" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/862f04d6-16e2-4a05-9961-c02515c776f5" />

I had a toy car that hadn’t worked for years because its remote had broken. I decided to take this opportunity to fix the car and remote while learning how to code my own remote control and upgrade the car with new components. I used Arduino boards, joysticks, nRF24L01 transmitter/receiver modules, an H-bridge motor driver, a power regulator, and an ultrasonic sensor. The joysticks allowed me to control the car’s movement, while the H-bridge controlled the direction and speed of the motors. I used the power regulator to provide the appropriate voltage to the different components, and the ultrasonic sensor to detect obstacles in front of the car. Since the car and remote needed to communicate wirelessly, I used the nRF24L01 modules to transmit control inputs from the remote to the car.
